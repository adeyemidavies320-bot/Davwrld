02 / The person behind the pixels

A CREATIVE MIND.
A PRACTICAL
APPROACH.

I’m Adeyemi Davies, also known as Mr Davies, an independent website designer based in Nigeria. I help businesses, founders and creatives turn their ideas into websites that feel professional, explain what they do and make the next step easy.

My work brings together design, development and attention to detail. I think about the structure of a page, the message behind each section and how the experience feels on a phone as carefully as how it looks on a desktop.

Webflow is a core part of my toolkit, alongside Wix, Shopify, WordPress, Framer and other website platforms. I can work from a Figma design, improve an existing website or build a new presence from the ground up. The platform follows your needs: a store, a service business, a portfolio or a more custom experience.

I’m also a university student, building my practice through hands-on work and continuous learning. I value clear conversations, useful feedback and websites that clients can confidently manage after launch.

07 / Before we get started

A FEW GOOD
QUESTIONS.

Have something else in mind? Tell me about your project and we’ll work through the details.

Which website platform should I choose?

It depends on your goals, how you want to manage content and the features you need. Shopify often suits dedicated online stores, while Webflow, Wix, WordPress and Framer offer different strengths for business websites. I’ll help you compare the options for your situation.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can review what’s working, what feels outdated and what needs improvement, then agree on a focused refresh or a more complete redesign.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your business goals, the pages or features you need, any brand materials, content and a few references you like. If some pieces are missing, we can identify what needs to be prepared before the build.

How much will my website cost?

Pricing depends on the number of pages, platform, design complexity, content and integrations. Share your brief and budget so I can propose a clear scope and quote before work begins.

Can I update the website after launch?

Yes. Where your platform supports it, I’ll organize editable content and provide guidance on common updates. We can also discuss ongoing support if you’d prefer help managing changes.
